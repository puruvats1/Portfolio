Working with me

01

Why before what, always.

Understanding root constraints before pushing pixels.

I never jump straight into interface solutions without first dissecting the underlying friction. I ask awkward questions, unpack user mental models, and dig through business and technical constraints.

02

Code-informed prototyping.

Bridging the chasm between static mockups and working code.

Static screens hide the tactile reality that only surfaces when humans touch an interface. Because I build functional prototypes in code, engineering remains aligned from day one.

03

Complexity is a playground.

Finding the hidden structure inside dense domains.

I naturally thrive in dense spaces like finance terminals, spatial tools, and developer platforms. I enjoy taking dozens of competing variables and structuring them into clear hierarchy.

04

Radical clarity, zero preciousness.

Design as an ongoing, transparent team dialogue.

Great work requires honest communication and zero ego. I communicate design rationale clearly, document systems thoroughly, and discard my own ideas without hesitation when a better solution emerges.
